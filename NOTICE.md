## Environment Agency LiDAR (derived terrain)

`data/terrain_horizon.js` and `data/site_ground_od.js` are **derived**
products built from Environment Agency LiDAR Digital Terrain Model
data. They are not the original EA rasters.

Environment Agency data is typically published under the
[Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/).

When reusing the derived terrain, please retain an attribution along
the lines of:

> Contains OS data © Crown copyright and database right.
> LiDAR data © Environment Agency copyright and/or database right.
> Derived mesh © Tim Daw (CC BY-SA 4.0).

Check current EA / Defra terms for the exact LiDAR product you use
when regenerating terrain.
